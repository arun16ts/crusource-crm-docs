## 8.1 Generative AI

| Feature | Short purpose | Verbatim details |
|---|---|---|
| **Module creation by Zia** | Create custom modules from natural language | "Zia can understand your natural language inputs and create modules with associated fields for you." |
| **Workflow creation by Zia** | Author workflows from NL prompt | "Tap into Zia's generative AI features to interpret your natural language commands and create workflows for you." |
| **Report creation by Zia** | Build reports from NL prompt | "Use natural language prompts to get Zia to create quick reports for you and take care of everything you'd typically do manually, like choosing modules, selecting fields, selecting filters, and defining charts." |
| **Record summary** | Summarise single record inline | "The record summary feature provides a condensed overview of a record's information without requiring extensive navigation through the record." |
| **Image to Canvas template** | Convert an image to a Canvas design | "Zia can analyze the structure and content to replicate it as a template for your CRM." |
| **Prompt builder** | Custom prompt invocations on buttons | "With the all-new prompt builder for custom buttons, you can create your own AI instructions to generate tailored content, suggestions, or summaries across record pages and list views." |
| **Smart prompt** | Email improvement tool | "Create new emails or improve existing ones, enhance language, and refine tone. You can also use smart prompts to generate or modify email templates, fetch valuable customer information from records, and summarize and enhance record notes." |

## 8.2 Predictions

| Feature | Short purpose | Verbatim or close paraphrase |
|---|---|---|
| **Churn prediction** | Predicts customer churn probability for each customer record. | "Zia predicts whether a customer will churn out of your business or not and displays a churn probability score for each customer record." |
| **Zia scores** | Lead/Deal conversion likelihood | "The Zia scores feature studies a particular record and assigns it a score... These scores represent the likelihood of conversion for a particular lead or deal." |
| **Field prediction** | Custom predictions | "Zoho CRM's field prediction enables you to build custom predictions that align with your business requirements... predict values for various business metrics, such as the likelihood of winning or losing a deal, the expected revenue from it." |
| **Prediction analytics** | Insight into model accuracy | "Zia's prediction analytics feature displays the data that was used as input to predict an outcome, such as the number of active predictions, prediction accuracy, the number of records involved in active predictions based on probability range, the number of records that uptrend or downtrend, and a time-based graphical representation." |
| **AI forecasting** | Goal/Target suggestion | "Zia suggests optimal targets for individual users and roles... Zia predicts how much an individual user or a team is likely to achieve in the current forecast period." |

## 8.12 Custom AI — QuickML

"QuickML: No-code machine learning pipeline builder service... to build, test, deploy, and monitor ML models for various business requirements." Ultimate edition users receive daily free QuickML calls (per the pricing page at https://www.zoho.com/crm/zohocrm-pricing.html).

## 8.13 Edition Gating (Zia)

Most AI features are tagged **Enterprise** or **Ultimate** in the marketing material unless labelled otherwise. **Best-time-to-contact analytics**, **churn prediction**, **next-best-experience**, **Zia scores** are typically enabled by default in **Professional+** for selected predictions. Custom AI / QuickML is reserved to **Ultimate**.
