# Module — Calendar

## CA.1 General Information

- **Aggregate view** of Tasks, Meetings, Calls.
- Calendar views available per activity module (https://help.zoho.com/portal/en/kb/crm/faqs/activity-management/articles/faqs-on-activities — verifies Calendar view is available for Meetings and Calls).

## CA.2 Views

- Day
- Week
- Month
- Agenda / List

## CA.3 Behaviour

- Drag-and-drop to reschedule.
- Color-coding per activity type (Tasks vs Meetings vs Calls).
