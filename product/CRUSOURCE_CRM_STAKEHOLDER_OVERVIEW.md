# Crusource CRM

## Product & Capability Overview

**Product:** Crusource CRM  
**Snapshot:** 26 September 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Crusource CRM is a multi-tenant customer relationship management platform designed to help sales and customer-facing teams manage the full journey from prospect discovery to customer engagement, revenue forecasting, and ongoing account work.

The platform brings customer records, sales pipelines, daily activities, email engagement, documents, collaboration, governance, and business insights into one permission-aware workspace. It is designed for sales representatives, managers, team leads, administrators, and organization members who need a shared and controlled view of customer work.

## Business Value

| Business need | Crusource CRM capability |
|---|---|
| Keep customer information organized | Central records for leads, contacts, accounts, deals, documents, notes, and activities |
| Give sales teams a clear daily work queue | Tasks, meetings, calls, calendar views, reminders, and dashboards |
| Improve pipeline visibility | Deal stages, values, expected close dates, forecasts, reporting, and analytics |
| Support consistent customer engagement | Email inbox integration, campaigns, templates, activity history, and collaboration tools |
| Enable responsible growth | Role-based permissions, team access controls, approvals, audit history, and administration tools |
| Reduce system switching | Integrated sales, communication, document, and planning workflows in one workspace |

## Core Product Modules

### Sales & Customer Management

| Module | What it enables |
|---|---|
| Leads | Capture, qualify, assign, route, organize, convert, import, and manage prospective customers. Teams can work in list or pipeline views. |
| Contacts | Maintain individual stakeholder profiles, communication details, account relationships, and engagement history. |
| Accounts | Manage customer organizations, account hierarchies, related people, deals, activities, documents, and customer context. |
| Deals | Track sales opportunities through configurable stages, expected revenue, close dates, products, owners, and outcomes. |
| Pipelines | Organize lead and deal progression through configurable stages and stage-history tracking. |
| Campaigns | Create and manage targeted email outreach, recipient groups, delivery progress, and campaign outcomes. |

### Daily Work & Customer Engagement

| Module | What it enables |
|---|---|
| Tasks | Plan and assign follow-up work with priorities, due dates, status, ownership, and bulk actions. |
| Meetings | Schedule meetings, capture agendas and outcomes, manage attendees and action items, and connect with Google Calendar. |
| Calls | Record customer calls, outcomes, duration, related records, and follow-up context. |
| Calendar | View work across tasks, meetings, and calls in calendar-based planning views. |
| Inbox & Email | Work with customer email threads and messages in the CRM, with Gmail-connected workflows where configured. |
| Notes | Capture global or record-specific notes, mentions, search, and contextual customer knowledge. |
| Notifications | Surface important user and system updates with read/unread management. |

### Insight, Planning & Decision Support

| Module | What it enables |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Give users a daily overview of key metrics, recent work, activities, and quick actions. |
| Analytics | Provide visual performance analysis, saved dashboards, KPI-oriented views, and drill-down experiences. |
| Reports | Create saved tabular, summary, and matrix reports with a live builder and query preview. |
| Forecast | Review pipeline value, weighted value, won value, stage distribution, and owner-level revenue outlook. |
| Search | Find relevant CRM records across supported modules through a centralized search experience. |

### Collaboration, Content & Knowledge

| Module | What it enables |
|---|---|
| Team Space | Support team lead pools, work distribution, record-access requests, reassignment workflows, and team dashboards. |
| Documents | Upload, organize, version, share, recover, and work with business documents and related customer files. |
| Templates | Create reusable document structures and support more consistent business documentation. |
| Approvals | Route controlled record-access and change requests for review, approval, or rejection. |

### Administration, Security & Governance

| Module | What it enables |
|---|---|

| Roles, Profiles & Permissions | Control who can view, create, edit, delete, export, import, or administer information by module and role. |

| Data Administration | Support organization-level data import, export, backup, storage, and recovery workflows. |
| Audit & Login History | Provide visibility into significant system activity, access history, and supported recovery/recycle-bin workflows. |

### Integrations & Intelligent Assistance

| Capability | What it enables |
|---|---|
| Google integration | Support Google sign-in, Gmail-connected workflows, and Google Calendar synchronization where configured. |
| File storage | Store and manage CRM-related documents through the platform’s storage integration layer. |
| AI assistance | Provide public and authenticated Loop AI experiences, chat history, streaming support, and CRM knowledge/embedding capabilities. |
| External connectivity | Provide integration-oriented setup and backend support for connected services used by the CRM. |

## Platform Characteristics

- **Multi-tenant:** Customer data is organized by organization, with scoped access controls.
- **Permission-aware:** Navigation, actions, records, and administrative capabilities are governed by user roles and permissions.
- **Workflow-oriented:** Customer records connect directly to the tasks, meetings, calls, notes, documents, and communications that move work forward.
- **Modular:** Sales, engagement, insight, collaboration, and governance capabilities can serve different teams while sharing one customer context.
- **Integration-ready:** The product includes support for connected services such as Google, email, calendar, file storage, and AI capabilities.

## Current Product Scope

Crusource CRM has a broad operational CRM foundation across sales records, activities, collaboration, documents, communication, access controls, and administration. Some advanced areas—including parts of analytics, reporting enhancements, forecasting configuration, broader data administration, AI-assisted CRM workflows, and automation administration—are actively evolving and should be positioned as expanding capabilities rather than universally complete workflows.

## Leadership Takeaway

Crusource CRM is positioned as a unified operating system for revenue and customer-facing teams: it helps teams organize customer information, execute daily sales work, collaborate securely, monitor performance, and make better-informed decisions as the organization grows.
