# Crusource CRM — User Stories by Role

## Everyone (Sales Rep, Sales Manager, Admin)

- Log in with my email and password into our company's single shared workspace.
- Land on a personal Home dashboard the moment I log in, automatically scoped to my role, showing my key numbers, open tasks, upcoming meetings, today's leads, and deals closing this month.
- Get to any part of the CRM in one click from the left-hand sidebar: Home, Workqueues, Reports, Sales (Leads, Contacts, Accounts, Deals, Documents), and Activities (Tasks, Meetings, Calls).
- Use a Workqueue to see a prioritized working list of what needs my attention.
- Search across Leads, Contacts, Accounts, and Deals at once from a single search bar, without needing to know which module a record lives in.
- Add a new Lead, Contact, Account, Deal, Document, Task, Meeting, or Call from anywhere using the quick-create (+) button, without leaving what I'm doing.
- Get notified when a task is due or something is assigned to me.
- See upcoming meetings and task deadlines at a glance from a calendar icon.
- Submit feedback or request a new feature directly from within the system.

## Sales Rep

- View, add, and manage my own leads, contacts, accounts, and deals.
- Import a batch of leads, contacts, accounts, or deals at once from a spreadsheet, instead of entering them one by one.
- Track a lead's status as it moves through New → Qualified → Converted, or mark it as Junk if it's not a real opportunity.
- Convert a qualified lead into an account, contact, and deal automatically.
- Filter and sort my leads, contacts, accounts, and deals to find what I need quickly (e.g. only open leads, or deals closing this month).
- Move deals through a clear, standardized six-stage pipeline (list view or drag-and-drop board), with each stage carrying a built-in win probability.
- See the total dollar value sitting in each stage on the pipeline board, and drag a deal from one stage to the next as it progresses — no forms required.
- Open an account's page and see everything connected to that company in one place — its contacts, deals, and activities — organized into tabs.
- Track each deal's next follow-up and last interaction, so I know what's due and what's gone quiet.
- Log tasks (with a status and priority level), meetings, and calls tied to each customer, with a full activity history.
- Record a meeting or call, or paste in my notes, and get an AI-generated summary plus a suggested next step — which I can turn into a follow-up task in one click.
- Add free-form notes to any lead, contact, account, or deal to capture quick context that doesn't fit elsewhere.
- Upload, view, and delete documents linked to an account or deal.
- Get an AI prompt asking me to confirm when I try to add a company that already exists, so I never create a duplicate account.
- Review previous conversations with a contact and get an AI-generated summary instead of scrolling through history.
- See each open lead's AI-generated score (0–100) with a plain-language reason, and sort my lead list by score so the highest-priority leads rise to the top.
- Get AI-drafted follow-up emails, and AI deal-risk alerts — with a short reason for each — that I can dismiss or clear by logging an activity, to work faster.
- Ask a conversational AI chatbot questions about my records in plain language, with answers linked back to their source.
- ***(Planned)*** Get daily task reminders, on-demand data summaries, and an automatic next-day Workqueue built from my end-of-day chat wrap-up, all through the chatbot.

## Sales Manager

- See everything my team owns — leads, contacts, accounts, deals, and activities — in addition to my own records.
- Track team-wide pipeline and performance on one dashboard.
- Open any individual rep's personal dashboard directly, and step in to perform Sales Rep actions on their behalf when needed.
- Assign tasks directly to reps on my team.
- Get alerted to stalled or at-risk deals across the team before they're lost.
- See each deal's next follow-up and last interaction across the team, so I know where to step in.
- Pull Lead, Deal, Activity, and Sales Rep Performance reports for my team without chasing reps for status updates.
- Use the same conversational AI chatbot to ask about any deal, contact, or account across my team instantly.

## Admin

- Add a new user to the organization and assign them a role — Admin, Sales Manager, or Sales Rep — right away.
- Edit a user's details or change their role as their responsibilities change.
- Deactivate a user to immediately cut off their login access, without deleting any of the records or history they created.
- Assign each Sales Rep to a Sales Manager, so team visibility rolls up correctly.
- Transfer a user's leads, contacts, accounts, deals, and activities to another user, so ownership stays clean when someone changes roles, changes territory, or leaves the company.
- See and manage every record across the organization.
- Control access so each person only sees what's relevant to their role.
- View an organization-wide analytics dashboard — open deals, pipeline by stage, leads this month, and activity stats — for a real-time read on overall sales health.
- Pull the same Lead, Deal, Activity, and Sales Rep Performance reports available across the organization.
- Customize how lead-scoring points are assigned, so scoring reflects what our organization values in a lead.
- Customize the pipeline's stage names, order, and win probabilities to match how we actually sell.
- ***(Planned)*** Roll out document version control and a custom report builder as they become available.
